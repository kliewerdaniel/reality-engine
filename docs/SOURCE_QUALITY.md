# Source Quality

**Reality Engine — Source Quality Document**
**Version:** 0.1.0
**Date:** November 1, 2026

This document defines source quality in Reality Engine. It covers how source quality is assessed without establishing a universal political, institutional, or editorial authority hierarchy.

---

## 1. Source Quality Definition

Source quality is the assessment of the reliability of a source for a specific type of information. It is multidimensional.

---

## 2. Quality Dimensions

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Source authenticity | Is the source authentic? |
| Source expertise | Is the source expert in the subject? |
| Directness | Is the evidence direct or secondhand? |
| Measurement quality | How reliable is the underlying measurement? |

**Constraint:** Do not collapse these into one universal reliability score. Domain-specific scoring may be introduced later, with explicit methodology.

---

## 2. Source Quality Assessment

The system must assess source quality when:

1. A new source is registered.
2. A source is used for a new domain.
3. A source's reliability is questioned.

---

## 3. Constraints

1. **Identity:** Each source quality record must have a stable unique identifier.
2. **Temporal:** Source quality has valid times.
3. **Provenance:** Each source quality record must have provenance.
4. **Versioning:** Source quality has versions.
5. **Consistency:** Source quality records must be consistent.
